📘 General Knowledge Quiz

1–5: Science

1. What is the chemical formula for water?

a) H₂O
b) CO₂
c) O₂
d) H₂

Answer: a) H₂O

2. Which part of the plant absorbs water and minerals from the soil?

a) Stem
b) Roots
c) Leaves
d) Flowers

Answer: b) Roots

3. What is the process by which plants make their own food?

a) Respiration
b) Photosynthesis
c) Decomposition
d) Fermentation

Answer: b) Photosynthesis

4. Which type of mirror is used in vehicles to provide a wider view?

a) Concave mirror
b) Convex mirror
c) Plane mirror
d) Spherical mirror

Answer: b) Convex mirror

5. What is the unit of measurement for electric current?

a) Volt
b) Ampere
c) Ohm
d) Watt

Answer: b) Ampere

6–10: Maths

6. What is the formula for the area of a rectangle?

a) Length × Width
b) Length + Width
c) Length - Width
d) Length ÷ Width

Answer: a) Length × Width

7. What is the sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral?

a) 180°
b) 360°
c) 90°
d) 270°

Answer: b) 360°

8. What is the formula for the volume of a cube?

a) Side × Side × Side
b) Side × Side
c) Side + Side + Side
d) Side - Side - Side

Answer: a) Side × Side × Side

9. What is the formula for the circumference of a circle?

a) 2πr
b) πr²
c) πd
d) 2πd

Answer: a) 2πr

10. What is the formula for the area of a triangle?

a) (Base × Height) / 2
b) Base × Height
c) (Base + Height) / 2
d) Base - Height

Answer: a) (Base × Height) / 2

15. What is the meaning of the word "सूर्य" in English?

a) Sun
b) Moon

16. What is the formula for calculating the mean?

a) Sum of observations / Number of observations
b) Sum of observations × Number of observations
c) Number of observations / Sum of observations
d) Sum of observations - Number of observations

Answer: a) Sum of observations / Number of observations

a) Ganges
b) Brahmaputra
c) Yamuna
d) Godavari

Answer: a) Ganges

18. What is the smallest state in India by area?

a) Goa
b) Sikkim

d) Mizoram

Answer: a) Goa

a) F. Scott Fitzgerald
b) Ernest Hemingway

Answer: a) F. Scott Fitzgerald

20. What is the meaning of the word "Democracy"?

a) Rule by one person
b) Rule by a few people
